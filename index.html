01

Before You Fly: Visa & Entry Documents

For a standard furniture-sourcing trip there is no visa to apply for. Australian citizens travelling on an ordinary passport may enter China visa-free for business, tourism, visiting relatives, exchange visits or transit for stays of up to 30 days, and this policy runs to 31 December 2026.

Bottom line

If your whole trip — Guangzhou arrival, Lecong furniture sourcing, factory visit, sightseeing — fits inside 30 days and falls within the visa-free purposes above, no visa application is required.

That is not the same as travelling with nothing but a passport. Immigration officers may ask you to show the purpose of your visit, so carry printed or on-screen copies of our invitation letter, your return flight booking and your hotel confirmation. Entry is always at the discretion of the border inspection officer.

If your stay exceeds 30 days, or your purpose falls outside the visa-free list, apply for the visa category that matches your actual purpose of visit — for commercial and trade activities that is the M visa. Check the current category list on the Chinese mission's website rather than assuming M always applies.

If you do need an M visa: two-stage online process

Since 15 September 2025 the Chinese missions in Australia use the China Visa Online System, so applications run as an online pre-review followed by an in-person document drop.

  1. Online pre-review. Go to www.visaforchina.cn, pick the visa centre for your state, complete the application form and upload every document. Review normally takes 3–5 working days after submission.
  2. In-person submission. Once pre-approved, present the original documents at the visa centre as instructed. Because fingerprint collection is waived for short-stay visas, someone else may submit on your behalf.
  3. Collection. Standard service is usually 4 working days; rush service is usually 2 working days. Express service (3 working days) is currently suspended. Postal applications take 10 working days excluding transit.

Plan on roughly 7–9 working days end to end (online pre-review plus standard processing), and start at least three weeks before departure.

On Arrival at Baiyun Airport: SIM, Mobile Pay, Cash

Three tasks in the arrivals hall, in this order. Together they take about 30 minutes and they unlock everything else — ride-hailing, scanning QR codes, paying in shops.

Step 1 · Buy a Chinese SIM card

Two dedicated service desks operate around the clock: the Comprehensive Service Centre for Overseas Visitors in the Level 1 international arrivals hall of Terminal 2 (communications zone), and the Facilitated Service Centre for Foreigners in the Level 1 international arrivals hall of Terminal 3. Both provide 24/7 staffed service, so a late-night arrival is not a problem.

Two hard rules

You must present your own valid passport (or foreign permanent residence ID) and attend in person — it cannot be done by proxy, so our greeter can accompany and translate but cannot lodge it for you. Allow about 15 minutes for real-name registration and facial verification.

If the queue is long, the alternative is a carrier store in the city: China Mobile 10086, China Unicom 10010, China Telecom 10000.

Official reference: Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, Guide for Inbound Foreign Visitors Applying for a Mobile SIM Card (2026 edition).

Step 2 · Link your foreign card to WeChat Pay or Alipay

Both wallets accept Visa and Mastercard issued outside China, both let you register with an overseas mobile number, and neither requires a Chinese bank account. On the standard published pricing there is no fee on a single transaction of ¥200 or less and 3% above ¥200. Tencent's 2026 announcement also introduced a waiver for users linking a foreign card for the first time, covering a continuous 90-day period and daily spending within ¥1,000 — whether it applies to you, and the exact rate, are shown in your own app, so treat the in-app figure as authoritative.

WeChat Pay

  1. Install or update WeChat, register or sign in with your overseas mobile number.
  2. Tap Me → Pay and Services → Wallet → Bank Cards → Add a Bank Card.
  3. Accept the service agreement.
  4. Enter identity details, choose Passport as the document type, upload the passport photo page.
  5. Add the card and complete SMS verification.
Limits — check your own app

Limits are not one fixed number for every visitor. They depend on your account status, the card you link and the wallet's risk controls. Tencent has published per-transaction limits of up to US$5,000 and annual limits of up to US$50,000 for verified international users, but the ceiling shown inside your own WeChat is the one that applies to you.

Foreign-card funding is intended for everyday consumption. Red packets and person-to-person transfers are generally not supported, so settle any goods payment to a supplier by bank transfer, and for a large single purchase ask the merchant to take your physical card on their terminal instead of relying on the wallet.

Alipay

  1. Install Alipay and switch to the international version; register with an overseas mobile number or email.
  2. Go to Account & Security → Real-Name Verification, upload your passport and complete facial recognition.
  3. Go to Bank Cards → Add Bank Card, enter card number, expiry and CVV, then complete the OTP check.

Accepted documents: passport, Mainland Travel Permits for Hong Kong and Macao residents, residence permits for Hong Kong and Macao residents, Mainland Travel Permit for Taiwan residents, or foreign permanent residence ID. Self-service setup takes about 5–10 minutes, excluding any delay on the issuing bank's side.

Fallback if card linking fails

Use Nihao China App from China UnionPay. It registers with an overseas mobile number and lets you top up a travel wallet directly from Visa, Mastercard or JCB cards, with no Chinese bank account needed. We suggest installing it before you leave Australia.

Step 3 · Exchange cash

Scan-to-pay covers most spending, but keep some cash for taxi tolls, small vendors and the occasional terminal that will not take a foreign card. Baiyun Airport has 4 licensed institutions and 10 counters across Terminals 1, 2 and 3 handling foreign currency cash exchange in more than 40 currencies including AUD. Opening hours vary by counter and by terminal — some operate around the clock while others run 06:00–24:00 or 06:30–23:00, so check the signboard at the counter you reach, particularly if you land late.

Counter locations

T1 departures hall 06:00–24:00 · T2 international departures landside 06:30–23:00 · T2 international departures airside 24 hours · T3 main building airside Level 4, 24 hours. One counter sits directly opposite the T2 overseas visitors service centre.

Limits

Self-service exchange machines: up to the equivalent of ¥5,000 per person per day. Same-day cash settlement up to the equivalent of US$10,000 needs only your passport; above that you must also present a customs declaration form or the original withdrawal receipt.

ATM and back-up

Airport ATMs dispense RMB against VISA, Mastercard and JCB cards 24/7. If a counter is under maintenance, use an ATM, or one of the 57 foreign-exchange counters inside star-rated hotels in Guangzhou — Sofitel, InterContinental and Hilton all qualify.

How much to exchange

At ¥200–300 of cash spending per day, roughly ¥1,500 covers a five-day trip. Settle everything large by card: it is safer, and you avoid losing value converting leftover RMB back on the way home.

Getting from the Airport to Lecong

The Louvre International Furniture Expo Center is at No. 3 Hebin South Road, Lecong Town, Shunde District — about 64 to 70 road kilometres from Baiyun Airport, crossing from Guangzhou into Foshan. Our factory is in Liangjiao Village in the same town, only 3 to 5 kilometres further on.

Worth checking first: the venue's own airport express

The Louvre operates its own city air terminal, and an airport express has been published as running direct to Terminals 2 and 1, terminating at the venue itself — no transfer and no address to explain. A reported fare is ¥39.8 for roughly 90 minutes, about one sixth of a ride-hail fare.

Please confirm the fare, departure times and terminal with us before you rely on it. The venue's published opening notice listed departures between 06:30 and 17:00 at roughly hourly intervals; that notice is historical and does not prove the same timetable still runs in 2026. If you land outside those hours the express will not be available. And like any road vehicle it is subject to the same traffic as a taxi, so it is not immune to congestion.

Reaching the Rattan Space factory

The factory is in Liangjiao Village, Lecong Town. The nearest metro stations are Shijilian and Dongping on the Guangfo Line, both 3 to 5 kilometres away, so the last leg is a short taxi of ¥10–20 taking 10 to 15 minutes. Direct from the airport, ride-hailing runs ¥190–240 including tolls, and a metered taxi ¥210–260 plus tolls.

Village industrial zones defeat navigation

Road naming and house numbering in the Liangjiao Village Third Industrial Zone are incompletely indexed in map apps, so a driver following the registered address will very likely stop in the wrong place. Please do not attempt this leg alone. We will send you the exact map pin and street-view photos before departure, and either meet you at Dongping station or send a car to collect you from the hotel.

Evening half-day

Canton Tower is one tram stop from Party Pier. Early morning, jet lag friendly

Baiyun Mountain is about 3 km from the city centre and the grounds are open 24 hours, so an early-morning walk up at 06:00–08:00 is possible and beats both the heat and the crowds. The cable car and sightseeing shuttle are a separate matter: the scenic area's published operating time is 09:00–18:00 and services may be suspended in bad weather, so do not plan a dawn ride. One timing warning

October is Canton Fair season. Road congestion around Canton Tower and Party Pier in the evening rises sharply and taxi times can exceed estimates. Sightseeing scheduled Monday to Thursday also earns the 25% intercity rail discount.

Typhoons

The Guangdong Climate Centre forecast on 30 September that a tropical cyclone making landfall on, or seriously affecting, Guangdong in October is unlikely, and Guangzhou meteorologists agree. The caution: the China Meteorological Administration noted on 29 September that three to four typhoons may still form in the western North Pacific and South China Sea in October, of which one or two could make landfall on or significantly affect the south China coast, with generally stronger intensity. As of 6 October three systems were active at sea with no direct impact on Chinese waters. Autumn typhoon tracks are highly uncertain, so buy travel insurance covering flight delay and start watching China Southern's flight status three days out.

Packing list

Clothing

  • 4–6 short-sleeve T-shirts or polo shirts — afternoons reach 28–33°C
  • 2–3 long-sleeve shirts for business meetings and evenings
  • One light jacket or cardigan — mornings and evenings drop to 18–22°C, and air conditioning in malls, venues, hotels and taxis is set low
  • One formal outfit for a private-room business dinner
  • One pair of comfortable walking shoes — Levels 1 to 6 of the Expo Center involve a great deal of walking

To carry

  • Folding umbrella — three separate rainfall events are forecast in October
  • Sunscreen SPF 20 or above with PA++, and sunglasses — ultraviolet is rated very strong
  • Face masks — autumn pollen and dry dust can push the allergy index to "prone to occur"; sensitive travellers should mask outdoors
  • Cold and flu remedies — four cold air incursions with frequent warm-cold swings make colds common

Air quality and health

Air quality has been good recently, though you should check a live AQI reading before planning outdoor time rather than relying on a historical average. In early October 2026 Guangzhou and Foshan readings were predominantly in the "excellent" band, with AQI mostly between 20 and 30 and PM2.5 below 20 µg/m³. Historically Guangzhou's October compliance rate is high, with light pollution only in the worst years — AQI 145 was recorded on 14 October 2022. Foshan reported 95.6% good-air days in the second quarter of 2026 with a mean PM2.5 of 18.2 µg/m³. On that record masks are more useful for autumn pollen than for pollution, but individual sensitivity varies.

Fire-danger season

October enters the low-rainfall period and the forest fire-danger meteorological rating is elevated, with home fire safety advised. For your itinerary this means woodland attractions such as Baiyun Mountain may restrict some areas or prohibit open flames when the rating rises — confirm opening status on the morning of your visit.

Four ways to bring the cost down

1. Airport express instead of ride-hailing — if it runs as published

A reported fare of ¥39.8 for about 90 minutes direct to the venue, against about ¥247 per car by ride-hail. Travelling alone that would be roughly ¥207 cheaper; with four sharing at about ¥62 each it is still around ¥22 cheaper per person. Two caveats: the fare and timetable are unconfirmed for 2026, and the bus drives the same roads as a taxi, so it offers no protection against congestion. Confirm before you count on the saving.

2. Intercity rail instead of the full metro route

The rail options differ by less than ¥30 but by about an hour: metro and bus ¥14 for 2 h 15 min, mixed metro and intercity from ¥19 for about 2 h, intercity about ¥41 for 60–70 min on the train. Paying ¥22–27 to buy back an hour is good value. Travel Monday to Thursday and the published fare drops a further 25%, saving about ¥10 on the ¥41 sector.

3. Sleep in Lecong, not in Guangzhou

Sofitel is reported as 200 m from the Expo Center and 15–20 minutes from our factory. A Tianhe base is 50–60 minutes away in each direction, and long taxi legs into Shunde pick up the 50% empty-return surcharge beyond 25 km. We are deliberately not quoting a total commuting figure here: the nightly rate gap between Lecong and Tianhe is roughly ¥470–800, and whether commuting outweighs it depends on how many days you actually travel into Guangzhou, the tolls on the day and whether you share cars. Pull a live in-app fare estimate for your own pattern before deciding — if you only visit Guangzhou once, the Lecong base is clearly ahead; if half your meetings are in the city, it may not be.

4. Combine cheap and free attractions

Baiyun Mountain ¥5 and Chen Clan Ancestral Hall ¥10 together cost ¥15, and the hall includes 11-language AI interpretation. Canton Tower from ¥150 is the only significant ticket, and pairing it with the free Party Pier on the same evening spreads that cost across a whole half-day.
